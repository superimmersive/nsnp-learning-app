## Full description

NSNP Learning is a short training app for school staff who run the National School Nutrition Programme.

It follows the 2026 roadshow for School Management Teams in the Eden and Central Karoo Education District. Principals, SMT members, NSNP coordinators, and food handlers can read the lessons on a phone, in the kitchen or the office, including when the signal is weak.

Eleven short modules cover:

- What the NSNP is, and who does what
- Documents and posters in the preparation area
- Volunteer food handlers, honoraria, and UIF
- The NSNP committee
- Monthly forms: feeding register, stock form, and proof of payment
- Menus, measurements, and feeding times
- Feeding with dignity
- Food safety and hygiene
- Deliveries and storage
- PPE, first aid, fire, and gas safety
- Daily feeding statistics

Each module ends with a five-question quiz. The pass mark is 4 out of 5. Checklists cover the preparation area, deliveries and storage, and the monthly forms. A deadlines page keeps 08:00, 12:30, 13:30, and the 7th of the month apart. A meal-quantity screen scales the roadshow example. It is an estimate. The official WCED calculator still wins before you order or cook.

Scores, checklist ticks, and the name on the learning record stay on the phone. This store version has no login and no account. It is a learning aid. It is not a WCED circular, not an official certificate, and not a way to submit feeding numbers to the district.

Lessons are in English.
