## Description

NSNP Learning is a short training app for school staff who run the National School Nutrition Programme.

It follows the 2026 roadshow for School Management Teams in the Eden and Central Karoo Education District. Principals, SMT members, NSNP coordinators, and food handlers can read the lessons on a phone, including when the signal is weak.

Eleven modules cover who does what, preparation-area documents, volunteer food handlers, the NSNP committee, monthly forms, menus and feeding times, feeding with dignity, food safety, deliveries and storage, safety equipment, and daily feeding statistics.

Each module ends with a five-question quiz. Checklists, a deadlines page, and a meal-quantity estimate sit beside the lessons. The estimate is not a substitute for the official WCED calculator.

Scores and the name on the learning record stay on the phone. There is no account and no login. The learning record is not an official certificate.

Lessons are in English.
